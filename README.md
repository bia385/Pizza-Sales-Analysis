# Pizza Sales Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate sales performance, identify best-selling products, and understand revenue trends across different pizza categories and sizes.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to present key performance indicators (KPIs) and sales insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and order volume.
- Calculate average revenue per order.
- Evaluate pizza sales by category.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling pizzas.
- Analyze revenue trends over time.
- Compare revenue across pizza categories and sizes.

## Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?
2. How many orders were received in total?
3. What is the average revenue per order?
4. How many pizzas were sold by category?
5. What are the top 10 best-selling pizzas?
6. How does revenue trend over time?
7. How does revenue vary by pizza category and size?

## Tools & Skills

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, data analysis, PivotTables, formulas, charts, and interactive dashboard development.
- **Data Analysis:** KPI calculation, product performance analysis, and time-series trend analysis.
- **Data Visualization:** Charts and interactive dashboard design.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Total Pizzas Sold
